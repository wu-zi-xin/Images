CCNA 1 - Module 1: Network Representations (1.3.1 網路表示法)

1.3.1 網路表示法 (Network Representations)
English
Network architects and administrators must be able to show what their networks will look like. They need to be able to easily see which components connect to other components, where they will be located, and how they will be connected. Diagrams of networks often use symbols to represent the different devices and connections that make up a network.
中文翻譯
網路架構師和管理員必須能夠展示他們網路未來的樣貌。他們需要能夠輕易看出哪些元件連接到其他元件、它們將位於何處，以及將如何連接。網路圖表通常使用符號來代表組成網路的不同設備與連線。
End Devices
(終端設備)
Desktop Computer (桌上型電腦), Laptop (筆記型電腦), Printer (印表機), IP Phone (IP電話), Wireless Tablet (無線平板), TelePresence Endpoint (網真端點)
Intermediary Devices
(中間設備)
Wireless Router (無線路由器), LAN Switch (區域網路交換器), Router (路由器), Multilayer Switch (多層交換器), Firewall Appliance (防火牆設備)
Network Media
(網路媒體)
Wireless Media (無線媒體), LAN Media (區域網路媒體), WAN Media (廣域網路媒體)
網路元件圖示類別 常見設備與代表符號名稱

💡 補充：專有名詞 (Specialized Terminology)
  • Network Interface Card (NIC / 網路介面卡): A NIC physically connects the end device to the network. (在實體上將終端設備連接到網路。)
  • Physical Port (實體連接埠): A connector or outlet on a networking device where the media connects to an end device or another networking device. (網路設備上的連接器或插座，用於將媒體連接到終端設備或其他網路設備。)
  • Interface (介面): Specialized ports on a networking device that connect to individual networks. Because routers connect networks, the ports on a router are referred to as network interfaces. (網路設備上連接到個別網路的專用連接埠。因為路由器是負責連接網路的，所以路由器上的連接埠通常被稱為網路介面。)
  • 註：術語「連接埠 (port)」和「介面 (interface)」在實務上經常交替使用。

CCNA 1 - 1.3.2 拓撲圖 (Topology Diagrams)

1.3.2 拓撲圖簡介與分類
English
Topology diagrams are mandatory documentation for anyone working with a network. They provide a visual map of how the network is connected. There are two types of topology diagrams: physical and logical.
中文翻譯
對於任何從事網路工作的人來說，拓撲圖是必備的說明文件。它們提供了網路如何連接的視覺地圖。拓撲圖分為兩種類型：實體拓撲與邏輯拓撲。
Physical Topology Diagrams
(實體拓撲圖)
English: Illustrate the physical location of intermediary devices and cable installation.

中文: 說明中間設備的「實體位置」和纜線安裝情況。在此類圖表中，您可以清楚看到設備所在的機房 (Room) 或機架 (Rack) 的標示。
Logical Topology Diagrams
(邏輯拓撲圖)
English: Illustrate devices, ports, and the addressing scheme of the network.

中文: 說明網路的設備、連接埠 (Ports) 以及「定址架構」(如 IP 位址/網段)。透過此圖，您可以看出哪些終端設備連接到哪些中間設備，以及正在使用的連線媒體。
