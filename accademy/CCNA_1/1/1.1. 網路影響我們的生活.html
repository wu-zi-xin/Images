CCNA 1 - Module 1: Networking Today (1.0 導言)

1.0.1 為什麼我應該學習這個模組？ (Why should I take this module?)
English
Welcome to Networking Today!
Congratulations! This module starts you on your path to a successful career in Information Technology by giving you a foundational understanding of the creation, operation, and maintenance of networks. As a bonus, you get to dive into networking simulations using Packet Tracer. We promise you will really enjoy it![cite: 4]
中文翻譯
歡迎來到今日網路！
恭喜！本模組透過帶領您建立對網路建立、運作與維護的基礎理解，為您的資訊科技（IT）成功職涯拉開序幕。作為額外收穫，您將深入使用 Packet Tracer 進行網路模擬。我們保證您一定會樂在其中！[cite: 4]
💡 額外補充與解析
CCNA 核心價值： Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA) 是網通領域最具代表性的入門認證之一。理解網路基礎架構是成為合格網通工程師的第一步。[cite: 4]
1.0.2 我將在這個模組學到什麼？ (What will I learn to do in this module?)

模組目標： Explain the advances in modern network technologies. (解釋現代網路技術的進步。)[cite: 4]

Topic Title (主題名稱) Topic Objective (主題目標中英對照)
Networks Affect our Lives Explain how networks affect our daily lives.
*(解釋網路如何影響我們的日常生活。)*[cite: 4]
Network Components Explain how host and network devices are used.
*(解釋主機與網路設備是如何被使用的。)*[cite: 4]
Common Types of Networks Compare the characteristics of common types of networks.
*(比較常見網路類型的特徵。)*[cite: 4]
Network Security Identify some basic security threats and solution for all networks.
*(識別所有網路的一些基本安全威脅與解決方案。)*[cite: 4]
1.0.4 Cisco Packet Tracer 入門
English
Packet Tracer is a tool that allows you to simulate real networks. It provides three main menus: add devices, select/delete/inspect components, and manage network profiles.[cite: 4]
中文翻譯
Packet Tracer 是一個允許您模擬真實網路的工具。它提供三個主要功能：新增設備、選取/刪除/檢查元件，以及管理網路設定檔。[cite: 4]
🛠️ Packet Tracer 常用功能提示
  • Open Samples: 開啟內建物聯網與網路設備預先建置範例。
  • Exit and Logout: 移除當前註冊資訊並要求重新登入。

CCNA 1 - 1.1.1 與 1.1.3 網路連結與無國界

1.1.1 網路將我們連結在一起 (Networks Connect Us)
English
Among all of the essentials for human existence, the need to interact with others ranks just below our need to sustain life. Communication is almost as important to us as our reliance on air, water, food, and shelter.[cite: 4]
中文翻譯
在人類生存的所有基本需求中，與他人互動的需求僅次於維持生命的需求。對我們而言，溝通的重要性幾乎僅次於我們對空氣、水、食物和住所的依賴。[cite: 4]
💡 實務補充
透過網路，新聞事件與新發現能在幾秒鐘內傳播至全世界，消除了實體距離的障礙。[cite: 4]
1.1.3 無國界 (No Boundaries)
English
The creation of the cloud let us store documents and pictures and access them anywhere, anytime. So whether we are on a train, in a park, or standing on top of a mountain, we can seamlessly access our data and applications on any device.[cite: 4]
中文翻譯
雲端的建立讓我們能夠隨時隨地儲存文件與圖片並進行存取。因此，無論我們是在火車上、公園裡，還是在山頂上，我們都可以在任何裝置上無縫存取我們的資料與應用程式。[cite: 4]
