1.10.1 What did I learn in this module? (網路基礎與組件)

網路組件 (Network Components)
English
All computers that are connected to a network and participate directly in network communication are classified as hosts. Intermediary devices connect the individual end devices to the network and can connect multiple individual networks to form an internetwork. The media provides the channel over which the message travels from source to destination.
中文翻譯
所有連接到網路並直接參與網路通訊的電腦都歸類為「主機 (Hosts)」。中間設備 (Intermediary devices) 將各個終端設備連接到網路，並能連接多個獨立網路以形成網際網路。媒介 (Media) 提供了訊息從來源傳輸到目的地的通道。
網路表示法與拓樸 (Representations and Topologies)
Physical topology
(實體拓樸)
Illustrates the physical location of intermediary devices and cable installation. (說明中間設備的實際實體位置和纜線安裝情況。)
Logical topology
(邏輯拓樸)
Illustrates devices, ports, and the addressing scheme of the network. (說明設備、連接埠以及網路的定址架構。)
拓樸類型 (Topology Type) 說明 (Description)

1.10.2 網路類型與連線 (Types & Connections)

常見的網路類型 (Common Types of Networks)
LAN
(Local Area Network / 區域網路)
A network infrastructure that provides access to users and end devices in a small geographical area. (在較小的地理範圍內提供使用者和終端設備存取的網路基礎設施。)
WAN
(Wide Area Network / 廣域網路)
A network infrastructure that provides access to other networks over a wide geographical area. (在廣闊的地理範圍內提供其他網路存取的網路基礎設施。)
Internet
(網際網路)
A worldwide collection of interconnected networks. (全球互連網路的集合體。)
Intranet & Extranet
(內部網路與外部網路)
Intranet: Private connection to an organization.
Extranet: Secure and safe access to individuals who work for a different organization (suppliers, customers). (Intranet 是組織的私人網路；Extranet 則提供給其他組織的人員如供應商或客戶安全存取。)

1.10.3 網路可靠性與趨勢 (Reliability & Trends)

可靠網路的四大特性 (Reliable Networks)
Fault Tolerance
(容錯能力)
Limits the number of affected devices during a failure. Allows multiple paths to a destination. (在發生故障時限制受影響的設備數量。允許到達目的地的多條路徑。)
Scalability
(可擴展性)
Expands quickly to support new users and applications without degrading performance. (能夠快速擴展以支援新的使用者和應用程式，而不會降低效能。)
Quality of Service (QoS)
(服務品質)
Primary mechanism for managing congestion and ensuring reliable delivery of content to all users. (管理壅塞並確保向所有用戶可靠傳遞內容的主要機制。)
Security
(安全性)
Three primary requirements: Confidentiality, Integrity, and Availability (CIA). (三個主要要求：機密性、完整性與可用性。)

網路趨勢 (Network Trends)
  • BYOD (Bring Your Own Device): Allows end users the freedom to use personal tools to access information. (允許終端使用者使用個人設備存取資訊)
  • Cloud Computing (雲端運算): Allows us to store personal files, even backup an entire drive. Includes Public, Private, Hybrid, and Custom clouds. (允許我們儲存個人檔案，甚至備份整個磁碟。包含公有雲、私有雲、混合雲等)
  • Smart Home Technology (智慧家庭): Technology integrated into everyday appliances connecting them to the network. (整合到日常家電中並將其連接到網路的技術)
  • WISP (Wireless Internet Service Provider): An ISP that connects subscribers to a designated access point or hot spot using wireless technologies. (使用無線技術將用戶連接到指定存取點或熱點的網際網路服務供應商)

1.10.4 模組測驗解析 (Module Quiz Review Q1-Q15)

以下整理了影片中完成的 15 題測驗重點，皆附有完整語音發音：

Q1
Spyware

Software secretly collecting data about visited websites.

(暗中收集使用者瀏覽網站紀錄的軟體)
Spyware (間諜軟體)
Q2
Extranet

Network providing secure access to corporate offices by suppliers, customers, and collaborators.

(提供供應商、客戶安全存取企業辦公室的網路)
Extranet (外部網路)

BYOD

Trend allowing users to access network resources from personal laptops and smartphones.

(允許使用者用個人筆電或手機存取網路資源的趨勢)
Bring your own device (BYOD)
Q4
ISP

What is an ISP?

(什麼是 ISP 網際網路服務供應商？)
It is an organization that enables individuals and businesses to connect to the Internet. (它是一個讓個人與企業能夠連接到網際網路的組織。)
Q5
WISP

Scenario where a WISP (Wireless ISP) is recommended.

(建議使用無線 ISP 的情境)
A farm in a rural area without wired broadband access. (沒有有線寬頻的鄉村農場)
Q6
Scalability

Characteristic enabling network to quickly grow without impacting performance.

(能讓網路快速增長且不影響效能的特性)
Scalability (可擴展性)
Q7
Fault Tolerance

Cable is cut, but users only experience very short interruption. What characteristic is this?

(纜線被切斷但只發生極短的斷線，這是什麼特性？)
Fault tolerance (容錯能力)
Q8
Scalable Network

Two characteristics of a scalable network.

(可擴展網路的兩個特徵)
Grows in size without impacting existing users Suitable for modular devices that allow for expansion (成長不影響現有用戶；適合允許擴展的模組化設備)
Q9
Router

Device determining the path messages should take.

(決定訊息傳遞路徑的設備)
A router (路由器)
Q10
Wireless Connections

Two Internet connection options not requiring physical cables.

(兩個不需要實體纜線的網路連線方式)
Cellular (行動網路) Satellite (衛星)
Q11
Online Shopping

Network must be accessed for online shopping.

(線上購物必須存取的網路)
The Internet (網際網路)
Q12
BYOD Impact

How BYOD changes businesses networks.

(BYOD 如何改變企業網路)
BYOD provides flexibility in where and how users can access network resources. (BYOD 為使用者存取網路資源的地點和方式提供了靈活性。)
Q13
VPN

Network feature allowing an employee to gain secure remote access.

(允許員工獲得安全遠端存取的網路功能)
VPN (虛擬私人網路)
Q14
Internet Definition

What is the Internet?

(什麼是網際網路？)
It provides connections through interconnected global networks. (它透過互連的全球網路提供連線。)
Q15
End Devices

Two functions of end devices.

(終端設備的兩個功能)
They originate the data that flows through the network. They are the interface between humans and the communication network. (產生網路資料；人與網路間的介面)
