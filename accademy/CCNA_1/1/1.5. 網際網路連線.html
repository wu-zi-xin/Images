CCNA 1 - Module 1.5: 1.5.1 & 1.5.2 家庭與小型辦公室網際網路連線

連線類型與常見技術
Cable
(有線電視寬頻)
English: Typically offered by cable television service providers, the internet data signal transmits on the same cable that delivers cable television. It provides a high bandwidth, high availability, and an always-on connection to the internet.

中文: 通常由有線電視服務供應商提供，網際網路資料訊號與傳輸有線電視的相同纜線一起傳輸。它提供高頻寬、高可用性且始終開啟的網際網路連線。
DSL
(數位用戶線路)
English: Digital Subscriber Lines also provide high bandwidth, high availability, and an always-on connection to the internet. DSL runs over a telephone line. In general, small office and home office users connect using Asymmetrical DSL (ADSL), which means that the download speed is faster than the upload speed.

中文: 數位用戶線路同樣提供高頻寬、高可用性且始終開啟的網際網路連線。DSL 透過電話線運行。一般來說，小型辦公室和家庭辦公室使用者使用非對稱 DSL (ADSL) 進行連線，這意味著下載速度快於上傳速度。
Cellular
(行動網路)
English: Cellular internet access uses a cell phone network to connect. Wherever you can get a cellular signal, you can get cellular internet access. Performance is limited by the capabilities of the phone and the cell tower to which it is connected.

中文: 行動網際網路存取使用手機網路進行連線。只要有行動訊號的地方，就可以獲得行動網際網路連線。效能受限於手機和所連接的基地台的功能。
Satellite
(衛星)
English: The availability of satellite internet access is a benefit in those areas that would otherwise have no internet connectivity at all. Satellite dishes require a clear line of sight to the satellite.

中文: 衛星網際網路存取的可用性，對於那些原本完全沒有網際網路連線的地區是一大福音。衛星天線需要有清晰的視線才能對準衛星。
Dial-up Telephone
(撥接電話)
English: An inexpensive option that uses any phone line and a modem. The low bandwidth provided by a dial-up modem connection is not sufficient for large data transfer, although it is useful for mobile access while traveling.

中文: 一種便宜的選項，使用任何電話線和數據機。撥接數據機連線提供的低頻寬不足以進行大型資料傳輸，但對於旅行時的行動存取很有用。

CCNA 1 - 1.5.3 企業網際網路連線 (Businesses Internet Connections)

企業級連線選項
Dedicated Leased Line
(專屬租用線路 / 專線)
English: Leased lines are reserved circuits within the service provider's network that connect geographically separated offices for private voice and/or data networking. The circuits are rented at a monthly or yearly rate.

中文: 專線是服務供應商網路中的保留電路，用於連接地理上分散的辦公室，以進行私人語音和/或資料網路傳輸。這些電路以月租或年租方式租用。
Metro Ethernet
(都會乙太網路)
English: This is sometimes known as Ethernet WAN. In this module, we will refer to it as Metro Ethernet. Metro ethernets extend LAN access technology into the WAN. Ethernet is a LAN technology you will learn about in a later module.

中文: 這有時被稱為乙太網路 WAN。在本模組中，我們將其稱為都會乙太網路。都會乙太網路將區域網路 (LAN) 存取技術擴展到廣域網路 (WAN)。乙太網路是一種您將在後續模組中學習的 LAN 技術。
Business DSL
(企業級 DSL)
English: Business DSL is available in various formats. A popular choice is Symmetric Digital Subscriber Line (SDSL) which is similar to the consumer version of DSL but provides uploads and downloads at the same high speeds.

中文: 企業級 DSL 提供多種格式。一個受歡迎的選擇是對稱數位用戶線路 (SDSL)，它類似於消費者版本的 DSL，但提供相同高速度的上傳和下載。
Satellite
(衛星)
English: Satellite service can provide a connection when a wired solution is not available.

中文: 當無法使用有線解決方案時，衛星服務可以提供連線。

CCNA 1 - 1.5.4 融合網路 (The Converging Network)

傳統獨立網路 (Traditional Separate Networks)
English
Consider a school built thirty years ago. Back then, some classrooms were cabled for the data network, telephone network, and video network for televisions. These separate networks could not communicate with each other. Each network used different technologies to carry the communication signal. Each network had its own set of rules and standards to ensure successful communication. Multiple services ran on multiple networks.
中文翻譯
想想一所三十年前建成的學校。那時候，有些教室分別鋪設了資料網路、電話網路和用於電視的視訊網路纜線。這些獨立的網路無法互相通訊。每個網路使用不同的技術來傳輸通訊訊號，且各自有一套規則和標準來確保通訊成功。多項服務在多個網路上獨立運作。
融合網路 (Converged Networks)
English
Today, the separate data, telephone, and video networks converge. Unlike dedicated networks, converged networks are capable of delivering data, voice, and video between many different types of devices over the same network infrastructure. This network infrastructure uses the same set of rules, agreements, and implementation standards. Converged data networks carry multiple services on one network.
中文翻譯
現今，獨立的資料、電話和視訊網路已經融合。與專用網路不同，融合網路能夠在同一網路基礎設施上，在許多不同類型的設備之間傳輸資料、語音和視訊。這個網路基礎設施使用相同的一套規則、協定和實作標準。融合資料網路在單一網路上承載多項服務。
