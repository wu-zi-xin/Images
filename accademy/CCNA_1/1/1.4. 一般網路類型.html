CCNA 1 - Module 1: Common Types of Networks (1.4.1 各種規模的網路)

1.4.1 各種規模的網路 (Networks of Many Sizes)
English
Networks come in all sizes. They range from simple networks consisting of two computers, to networks connecting millions of devices.
中文翻譯
網路有各種不同的規模。它們從僅由兩台電腦組成的簡單網路，到連接數百萬台設備的網路都有。
Small Home Networks
(小型家庭網路)
Connect a few computers to each other and to the internet. (將少數幾台電腦互相連接並連上網際網路。)
Small Office and Home Office Networks (SOHO)
(小型辦公室與家庭辦公室)
The SOHO network allows computers in a home office or a remote office to connect to a corporate network, or access centralized, shared resources. (SOHO 網路允許家庭辦公室或遠端辦公室的電腦連接到企業網路，或存取集中且共用的資源。)
Medium to Large Networks
(中大型網路)
Such as those used by corporations and schools, can have many locations with hundreds or thousands of interconnected hosts. (例如企業與學校所使用的網路，可能擁有多個據點，並連接數百或數千台主機。)
World Wide Networks
(全球性網路)
The internet is a network of networks that connects hundreds of millions of computers world-wide. (網際網路是一個「網路中的網路」，連接全球數以億計的電腦。)

CCNA 1 - 1.4.2 區域網路與廣域網路 (LANs and WANs)

1.4.2 基礎網路架構類型
English
The two most common types of network infrastructures are Local Area Networks (LANs), and Wide Area Networks (WANs).
中文翻譯
兩種最常見的網路基礎架構類型是區域網路 (LAN) 和廣域網路 (WAN)。
LANs
(區域網路)
  • 範圍： Interconnect end devices in a limited area such as a home, school, office building, or campus. (在有限的區域內互連終端設備，例如家庭、學校、辦公大樓或校園。)
  • 管理： Usually administered by a single organization or individual. (通常由單一組織或個人管理。)
  • 頻寬： Provide high-speed bandwidth to internal end devices and intermediary devices. (為內部終端設備與中間設備提供高速頻寬。)
WANs
(廣域網路)
  • 範圍： Interconnect LANs over wide geographical areas such as between cities, states, provinces, countries, or continents. (在廣泛的地理區域間互連區域網路，例如城市、州、省、國家或洲之間。)
  • 管理： Usually administered by multiple service providers. (通常由多個服務提供商管理。)
  • 頻寬： Typically provide slower speed links between LANs. (通常在區域網路之間提供速度較慢的連線。)
網路類型 主要特徵與說明 (Characteristics)
LANs
(區域網路)

  • 管理： Usually administered by a single organization or individual. (通常由單一組織或個人管理。)
  • 頻寬： Provide high-speed bandwidth to internal end devices and intermediary devices. (為內部終端設備與中間設備提供高速頻寬。)
WANs
(廣域網路)
  • 範圍： Interconnect LANs over wide geographical areas such as between cities, states, provinces, countries, or continents. (在廣泛的地理區域間互連區域網路，例如城市、州、省、國家或洲之間。)
  • 管理： Usually administered by multiple service providers. (通常由多個服務提供商管理。)
  • 頻寬： Typically provide slower speed links between LANs. (通常在區域網路之間提供速度較慢的連線。)

CCNA 1 - 1.4.3 網際網路 (The Internet)

1.4.3 The Internet 簡介
English
The internet is a worldwide collection of interconnected networks (internetworks, or internet for short). The internet is not owned by any individual or group.
中文翻譯
網際網路是一個由互相連接的網路所組成的全球性集合體 (簡稱 internetworks 或 internet)。網際網路不屬於任何個人或團體。
💡 補充：網路標準組織

Ensuring effective communication across this diverse infrastructure requires the application of consistent and commonly recognized technologies and standards. 為了確保全球網路能有效通訊，需要應用一致且公認的技術與標準。負責維護網路結構與標準化的組織包含：

  • IETF: Internet Engineering Task Force (網際網路工程任務編組)
  • ICANN: Internet Corporation for Assigned Names and Numbers (網際網路名稱與數字位址分配機構)
  • IAB: Internet Architecture Board (網際網路架構委員會)

CCNA 1 - 1.4.4 內部網路與外部網路 (Intranets and Extranets)

1.4.4 網路存取權限的層次
Intranet
(內部網路)
Company Only (僅限公司內部)
A term often used to refer to a private connection of LANs and WANs that belongs to an organization. Designed to be accessible only by the organization's members, employees, or others with authorization. (通常指屬於某個組織的區域網路與廣域網路的私有連線。設計上僅供該組織的成員、員工或其他授權人員存取。)
Extranet
(外部網路)
Suppliers, Customers, Collaborators (供應商、客戶、合作夥伴)
An organization may use an extranet to provide secure and safe access to individuals who work for a different organization but require access to the organization's data. (組織可能使用外部網路，以提供安全可靠的存取管道，給那些為不同組織工作但需要存取該組織資料的個人。)
The Internet
(網際網路)
The World (全世界)
Publicly accessible global network. (公開可存取的全球網路。)
