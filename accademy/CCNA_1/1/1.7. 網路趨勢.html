CCNA 1 - Module 1: Network Trends (1.7.1 近期趨勢)

1.7.1 近期趨勢 (Recent Trends)
English
As new technologies and end-user devices come to market, businesses and consumers must continue to adjust to this ever-changing environment. There are several networking trends that affect organizations and consumers:
  • Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)
  • Online collaboration
  • Video communications
  • Cloud Computing
中文翻譯
隨著新技術和終端使用者設備進入市場，企業和消費者必須繼續適應這個不斷變化的環境。有幾個影響組織和消費者的網路趨勢：
  • 自攜設備 (Bring Your Own Device, BYOD)
  • 線上協作 (Online collaboration)
  • 視訊通訊 (Video communications)
  • 雲端運算 (Cloud Computing)

CCNA 1 - 1.7.2 自攜設備 (BYOD)

1.7.2 自攜設備 (BYOD) 簡介
English
The concept of any device, for any content, in any manner, is a major global trend that requires significant changes to the way we use devices and safely connect them to networks. This is called Bring Your Own Device (BYOD).

BYOD enables end users the freedom to use personal tools to access information and communicate across a business or campus network. BYOD means any device, with any ownership, used anywhere.
中文翻譯
「任何設備、任何內容、任何方式」的概念，是一個主要的全球趨勢，這需要我們對使用設備以及安全連接網路的方式進行重大改變。這被稱為自攜設備 (BYOD)。

BYOD 賦予終端使用者自由，讓他們可以使用個人工具在企業或校園網路中存取資訊並進行通訊。BYOD 意味著在任何地方使用任何擁有權的任何設備。

CCNA 1 - 1.7.3 線上協作 (Online Collaboration)

1.7.3 線上協作簡介
English
Individuals want to connect to the network, not only for access to data applications, but also to collaborate with one another. Collaboration is defined as "the act of working with another or others on a joint project."

Collaboration is a critical and strategic priority that organizations are using to remain competitive. Cisco Webex Teams is a multifunctional collaboration tool that lets you send instant messages, post images, and post videos and links.
中文翻譯
個人希望連接到網路，不僅是為了存取資料應用程式，也是為了與他人協作。協作被定義為「與另一個人或多個人在共同專案上合作的行為」。

協作是組織用來保持競爭力的關鍵戰略優先事項。Cisco Webex Teams 是一種多功能的協作工具，可讓您傳送即時訊息、張貼圖片，以及張貼影片和連結。

CCNA 1 - 1.7.4 視訊通訊 (Video Communications)

1.7.4 視訊通訊簡介
English
Another facet of networking that is critical to the communication and collaboration effort is video. Video is used for communications, collaboration, and entertainment. Video calls are made to and from anyone with an internet connection, regardless of where they are located.

Video conferencing is a powerful tool for communicating with others, both locally and globally.
中文翻譯
對通訊和協作至關重要的另一個網路層面是視訊。視訊用於通訊、協作和娛樂。無論身在何處，只要有網路連線，任何人都可以撥打和接聽視訊通話。

視訊會議是與本地和全球其他人進行交流的強大工具。

CCNA 1 - 1.7.6 雲端運算 (Cloud Computing)

1.7.6 雲端運算與資料中心
English
Cloud computing is one of the ways that we access and store data. Cloud computing allows us to store personal files, even backup an entire drive on servers over the internet.

Cloud computing is possible because of data centers. For security, reliability, and fault tolerance, cloud providers often store data in distributed data centers.
中文翻譯
雲端運算是我們存取和儲存資料的方式之一。雲端運算允許我們儲存個人檔案，甚至透過網際網路在伺服器上備份整個磁碟機。

雲端運算之所以可能，是因為有資料中心。為了安全性、可靠性和容錯能力，雲端提供商通常將資料儲存在分散式的資料中心。
Public clouds
(公有雲)
Cloud-based applications and services offered in a public cloud are made available to the general population. (在公有雲中提供的基於雲端的應用程式和服務可供大眾使用。服務可能是免費或按使用付費。)
Private clouds
(私有雲)
Intended for a specific organization or entity, such as a government. (專為特定組織或實體（如政府）而設。可使用組織的私有網路建立。)
Hybrid clouds
(混合雲)
Made up of two or more clouds (example: part private, part public), where each part remains a distinct object, but both are connected using a single architecture. (由兩個或多個雲端組成（例如：部分私有，部分公有），每個部分保持獨立，但使用單一架構連接。)
Community clouds
(社群雲)
Created for exclusive use by specific entities or organizations with similar needs and concerns, like healthcare organizations. (專供有類似需求和顧慮的特定實體或組織（如醫療機構）專屬使用。)

CCNA 1 - 1.7.7 家庭科技趨勢 (Technology Trends in the Home)

1.7.7 智慧家庭科技 (Smart Home Technology)
English
Networking trends are not only affecting the way we communicate at work and at school, but also changing many aspects of the home. The newest home trends include 'smart home technology'.

Smart home technology integrates into every-day appliances, which can then connect with other devices to make the appliances more 'smart' or automated.
中文翻譯
網路趨勢不僅影響我們在工作和學校的通訊方式，也改變了家庭的許多層面。最新的家庭趨勢包含「智慧家庭科技」。

智慧家庭科技整合到日常家電中，這些家電可以與其他設備連接，使家電變得更加「智慧」或自動化。

CCNA 1 - 1.7.8 電力線網路 (Powerline Networking)

1.7.8 電力線網路簡介
English
Powerline networking for home networks uses existing electrical wiring to connect devices.

Using a standard powerline adapter, devices can connect to the LAN wherever there is an electrical outlet. No data cables need to be installed. Powerline networking is especially useful when wireless access points cannot reach all the devices in the home.
中文翻譯
家庭網路的電力線網路使用現有的電線來連接設備。

使用標準的電力線網路卡，設備只要在有電源插座的地方就能連接到區域網路 (LAN)。不需要安裝數據纜線。當無線存取點無法覆蓋家中所有設備時，電力線網路特別有用。

CCNA 1 - 1.7.9 無線寬頻 (Wireless Broadband)

1.7.9 無線寬頻與 WISP
English
In many areas where cable and DSL are not available, wireless may be used to connect to the internet.

Wireless Internet Service Provider (WISP) is an ISP that connects subscribers to a designated access point or hot spot using similar wireless technologies found in home wireless local area networks (WLANs).

Wireless Broadband Service uses the same cellular technology as a smart phone. An antenna is installed outside the house providing either wireless or wired connectivity for devices in the home.
中文翻譯
在許多無法使用有線電視和 DSL 寬頻的地區，可以使用無線技術來連接網際網路。

無線網際網路服務提供商 (WISP) 是一種 ISP，它使用家庭無線區域網路 (WLAN) 中常見的類似無線技術，將訂戶連接到指定的存取點或熱點。

無線寬頻服務 則使用與智慧型手機相同的行動通訊技術。在屋外安裝天線，為屋內的設備提供無線或有線連線。
