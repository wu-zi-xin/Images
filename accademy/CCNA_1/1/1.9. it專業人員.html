CCNA 1 - Module 1: The IT Professional (1.9.1 CCNA)

IT 職涯與技能準備 (IT Career and Skills)
English
As a NetAcad student, you may already have a career in IT, or are still educating yourself to prepare for your career. In either case, it is good to know about the skills needed to match the types of jobs that are available in IT.
中文翻譯
身為 NetAcad 學生，您可能已經在 IT 行業工作，或者仍在進修以準備您的職涯。無論是哪種情況，了解符合 IT 領域現有工作類型所需的技能都是件好事。
CCNA 認證的價值 (Value of CCNA Certification)
English
The role and skills required of network engineers are evolving and are more vital than ever. The Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA) certification demonstrates that you have a knowledge of foundational technologies and ensures you stay relevant with skill sets needed for the adoption of next generation technologies.
中文翻譯
網路工程師所需的角色和技能正在不斷發展，並且比以往任何時候都更加重要。思科認證網路工程師 (CCNA) 證明您具備基礎技術的知識，並確保您掌握採用新一代技術所需的相關技能。
整合更新的 CCNA 內容 (Updated CCNA Topics)
Consolidated curriculum
(整合的課程)
A consolidated and updated CCNA for networking engineers is three courses and one exam which covers the fundamental topics for all network technologies. (針對網路工程師，經過整合與更新的 CCNA 包含三門課程和一項考試，涵蓋了所有網路技術的基礎主題。)
New focus areas
(新的重點領域)
The new CCNA focuses on IP foundation and security topics along with wireless, virtualization, automation, and network programmability. (新的 CCNA 專注於 IP 基礎和安全主題，以及無線網路、虛擬化、自動化和網路可程式化。)
認證重點 (Certification Focus) 說明 (Description)
Consolidated curriculum (整合的課程) A consolidated and updated CCNA for networking engineers is three courses and one exam which covers the fundamental topics for all network technologies.

其他思科認證選項 (Other Cisco Certifications)
English
There are new DevNet certifications at the associate, specialist and professional levels, to validate your software development skills.

There are specialist certification options to validate your skills in line with your job role and interests. This includes the Cisco Enterprise Advanced Infrastructure Specialist certification.

You can start where you want. There are no prerequisites to start earning your associate, specialist, professional, or expert level certification. Continuing education credits for recertification and ongoing development are now available for CCNA.
中文翻譯
有新的 DevNet 認證包含助理級、專家級和專業級，以驗證您的軟體開發技能。

還有專家級認證選項可以驗證符合您職位角色和興趣的技能。這包括思科企業進階基礎設施專家認證。

您可以從想開始的地方開始。取得您的助理級、專家級、專業級或專家級認證沒有任何先決條件。現在 CCNA 也能透過持續教育學分進行重新認證和持續進修。

CCNA 1 - 1.9.2 網路工作 (Networking Jobs)

尋找就業機會 (Finding Employment Opportunities)
English
Your CCNA certification will prepare you for a variety of jobs in today's market. At www.netacad.com you can click the Careers menu and then select Employment opportunities. You can find employment opportunities where you live by using the new program, the Talent Bridge Matching Engine. Search for jobs with Cisco, as well as Cisco partners and distributors seeking Cisco Networking Academy students and alumni.
中文翻譯
您的 CCNA 認證將為您在當今市場上的各種工作做好準備。在 www.netacad.com 上，您可以點擊「職涯 (Careers)」選單，然後選擇「就業機會 (Employment opportunities)」。您可以使用新計畫「Talent Bridge 匹配引擎」找到您居住地的就業機會。搜尋思科，以及尋求思科網路學院學生和校友的思科合作夥伴與經銷商的工作。
使用線上搜尋引擎 (Online Search Engines)
English
You can also search for IT jobs using online search engines such as Indeed, Glassdoor, and Monster. Use search terms such as IT, network administrator, network architects, and computer systems administrator. You can also search using the term Cisco CCNA.
中文翻譯
您也可以使用 Indeed、Glassdoor 和 Monster 等線上搜尋引擎尋找 IT 工作。使用搜尋詞彙如 IT、網路管理員 (network administrator)、網路架構師 (network architects) 和電腦系統管理員 (computer systems administrator)。您也可以直接使用搜尋詞彙「Cisco CCNA」。

CCNA 1 - 1.9.3 實驗 (Lab)

1.9.3 Lab - Research IT and Networking Job Opportunities

In this lab, you will complete the following objectives:
(在這個實驗中，您將完成以下目標：)

Part 1: Research Job Opportunities
(第一部分：研究工作機會)
Use online search engines and job boards to explore currently available positions in the IT and networking field. (使用線上搜尋引擎與求職平台，探索 IT 與網路領域目前開放的工作職缺。)
Part 2: Reflect on Research
(第二部分：反思研究結果)
Analyze the skills and requirements requested by employers and see how they align with the CCNA curriculum. (分析雇主所要求的技能與條件，並檢視它們與 CCNA 課程內容的關聯性。)
