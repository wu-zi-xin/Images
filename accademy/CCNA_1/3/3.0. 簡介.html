3.0.1 Why should I take this module? (為何我要學習本模組？)

Welcome to Protocols and Models!

您已經了解了簡單網路的基本元件以及初始設定。但是在您設定並連接好這些元件後，您如何確定它們能共同運作？這就需要透過協定與模型。

Protocols (協定)
Protocols are sets of agreed upon rules that have been created by standards organizations. 協定是由標準組織所建立的共同約定規則集合。
Models (模型)
Because you cannot pick up a rule and look closely at it, how do you truly understand why there is such a rule and what it is supposed to do? Models give you a way to visualize the rules and their place in your network. 因為您無法實際把「規則」拿起來仔細觀察，所以要如何真正理解為何需要這個規則以及它的作用？模型提供了一種方法，讓您將規則及其在網路中所處的位置「視覺化」。
💡 章節總覽：
This module gives you an overview of network protocols and models. You are about to have a much deeper understanding of how networks actually work!
(本模組將帶您概覽網路協定與模型。您即將對網路的實際運作方式有更深入的了解！)

3.0.3 Class Activity - Design a Communications System

課堂活動：設計通訊系統

本活動透過修車的情境，隱喻網路通訊中「協定 (Protocols)」的重要性：

Background (背景情境)
You have just purchased a new automobile for your personal use. After driving the car for a week or so, you find that it is not working correctly. Discussing the problem with several of your peers, you decide to take it to an automotive repair facility that they highly recommend. It is the only repair facility located in close proximity. 您剛買了一輛私人用的新車。開了約一週後，您發現它運作不太正常。與幾位同儕討論問題後，您決定把它送到他們極力推薦的一家汽車維修廠。這是附近唯一的一家維修廠。
The Problem (面臨問題)
When you arrive at the repair facility, you find that all the mechanics speak another language. You are having difficulty explaining the automobile's performance problems, but the repairs really need to be done. You are not sure you can drive it back home to research other options. 當您抵達維修廠時，您發現所有的技師都說著另一種語言。您很難向他們解釋汽車的效能問題，但車子又非修不可。您不確定是否能把它開回家再找其他選擇。
The Task (任務目標)
You must find a way to work with the repair facility to ensure your automobile is fixed correctly.

How will you communicate with the mechanics? Design a communications model to ensure that the car is properly repaired. 您必須找到一種方法與維修廠合作，以確保您的汽車能被正確修復。

您將如何與技師溝通？設計一個通訊模型，以確保汽車能得到妥善維修。
💡 思考方向：
這個活動暗示了網路世界中，來自不同製造商的設備 (就像說不同語言的人) 需要一個共通的語言標準 (通訊協定)，才能互相理解並完成任務。
